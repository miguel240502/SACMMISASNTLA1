Track Topics

We invite original research contributions, innovative applications, and case studies in the following areas (but not limited to):

Artificial Intelligence
  • Machine learning and deep learning applications
  • Natural language processing and text mining
  • Pattern recognition and computer vision
  • Knowledge engineering and semantic web
Data Engineering
  • Data science and information systems
  • Real-time data processing and streaming analytics
  • Data Quality and Governance
Optimization & Systems
  • Intelligent algorithms and optimization techniques
  • Decision support systems and recommender systems
    • Bio-Inspired & Fuzzy Systems
    • Evolutionary computation and bio-inspired algorithms
    • Fuzzy logic and soft computing
    Human & Social Aspects
    • Human-Computer Interaction
    • Social media analytics and sentiment analysis
    • Plagiarism detection and authorship analysis
    Submission Guidelines
    • Papers must be written in English and not exceed 12 pages (including figures and appendices). Contributions should include unpublished results of research, case studies or experiences that provide new evidence about the research or application regarding to the main topics.
    • Maximum of 4 authors per manuscript.
    • All manuscripts will undergo a double-blind peer review by at least two reviewers.
    • Please ensure that your papers are formatted correctly using the provided latex template, word template.
    • Authors must submit the PDF generated from LaTeX through the submission system.
    Proceedings (ISSN: 2594-1054)

    All accepted papers will be published in the Conference Proceedings (ISSN: 2594-1054) and will be available to the scientific community.

    Selected high‑quality papers may be invited for submission to JCR Journals of Combinatorial Optimization Problems and informatics (IJCOPI). These submissions will undergo the IJCOPI journal’s peer-review process. Articles must be extended versions in English, including at least 30% new and original content compared to what was published in the conference proceedings (this may include changes in the introduction, title, abstract, conclusions, among others). These articles will be subject to an additional fee established by the IJCOPI journal.

    Submit via CMT-3
