What is SMaDE 2025?

The International Congress on Smart Machines and Data Engineering (SMaDE) is an international forum dedicated to presenting and discussing cutting-edge research, methodologies, and applications in artificial intelligence, algorithms, and data-driven technologies. It will take place on Novembre 27–28, 2025 in Coatzacoalcos, Veracruz, Mexico.

SMaDE brings together researchers, professionals, students, and industry leaders to exchange scientific results, foster collaboration, and explore emerging trends in intelligent systems and data engineering.
