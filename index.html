SMaDE 2025

International Congress on Smart Machines and Data Engineering

December 4–5, 2025 | Coatzacoalcos, Veracruz, Mexico

What is SMaDE 2025?

The International Congress on Smart Machines and Data Engineering (SMaDE) is an international forum dedicated to presenting and discussing cutting-edge research, methodologies, and applications in artificial intelligence, algorithms, and data-driven technologies. It will take place on December 4–5, 2025 in Coatzacoalcos, Veracruz, Mexico.

Track Topics

Including but not limited to

Artificial Intelligence
  • Machine learning and deep learning applications
  • Natural language processing and text mining
  • Pattern recognition and computer vision
Data Engineering
  • Big data analytics and data mining

  • Plagiarism detection and authorship analysis
Submission Guidelines
  • Papers must be written in English and not exceed 12 pages (including figures and appendices). Contributions should include unpublished results of research, case studies or experiences that provide new evidence about the research or application regarding to the main topics.
  • All manuscripts will undergo a double-blind peer review by at least two reviewers.
  • Please ensure that your papers are formatted correctly.
